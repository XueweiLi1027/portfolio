Social Science Studies
University of Chicago · 2024
What Drives Housing Prices in U.S. High-Poverty Neighborhoods? A Computational Social Science Study

A team final project for MACS 30112 at UChicago, examining whether housing attributes or neighborhood resources better explain prices in high-poverty tracts across NYC, LA, and Chicago. We scraped Redfin listings and joined them with city open-data layers (parks, transit, hospitals, crime). Regression analysis showed neighborhood attributes outweighed housing attributes overall, with each city showing distinct local preferences: Chicago favoring single-family residential, LA weighting business atmosphere, and NYC discounting hospital proximity.
